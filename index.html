The Studio

A studio with a point of view.

Chalk Studio is the newest chapter from Harmony Bidwell, the Portland cosmetologist and co-owner behind Slate Studio and Apotheosis Salon. Where Slate is bright, airy and industrial, Chalk was built to feel like the opposite: warm, layered and a little unexpected.

Think chalk-white walls traced in fine brass line-work, cognac leather chairs, walnut cabinetry, ochre velvet lounges and brass fauna keeping watch from the walls. It is refined, but never precious, a space with taste and a sense of humor.

Like its sister studio, Chalk runs on a booth-rental model. Every artist here is an independent business owner who sets their own hours, prices and style. Our job is simply to give them a beautiful room to do their best work in.

— Harmony Bidwell, Owner

What happens here

Every kind of hair, done well.

Because each chair is independently owned, exact menus and pricing vary by artist. Here is the range of work our stylists specialize in.

01

Cuts & Shapes

Precision and lived-in cuts for every texture and every gender. Bring a reference or bring a mood.

02

Color

Dimensional and live-in color, balayage, grey blending, corrections and the occasional fantastical melt.

03

Blondes & Foiling

Micro-foils, full highlights and lightening handled with care for the health of your hair.

04

Extensions

Custom extensions and installs to add length, fullness or a little drama, tailored to you.

05

Barbering

Classic and modern cuts, clipper work, beard trims and straight-razor shaves.

06

Occasion & Styling

Special-occasion hair, blowouts and smoothing treatments for weddings, events and everyday.
